Note Tube 5

Note Tube 5 ("YouTube 5" on the device) is a personal, non-commercial YouTube client for an old Android 5 phone. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

  • What it accesses: if you sign in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API to read your subscriptions, your playlists and your channel name/picture, and, only when you tap the button, to like/dislike a video, subscribe/unsubscribe, or post a comment on your behalf.
  • Where data is stored: only on your phone (watch history, playlists, settings, sign-in token). Nothing is sent to any server operated by the developer; there is no such server.
  • Sharing: your data is never sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is used only to show your own YouTube content to you.
  • Removing access: sign out in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
